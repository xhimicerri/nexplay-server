NEXPLAY Server lets you watch and listen to YOUR OWN media files (movies, series, music)
on your devices with the NEXPLAY apps. You are responsible for the content you add and for
having the right to use it.

Your media, accounts and settings stay on this computer. NEXPLAY Server does not send your
files or personal data anywhere. Optional: when you add a TMDB API key in Settings, the
server asks themoviedb.org for titles, posters and descriptions.
